The idea
One agent learns from you, one checks you
You
← explain
Sub-student
Starts from zero: it knows only what you teach it.
  • Asks naive questions so you go deeper
  • Doesn’t know the answers, so it can’t give them away
  • At the end, proves formally to the teacher what it understood
Teacher
Knows the course: the lecturer’s slides and notes.
  • Knows which concepts you haven’t covered yet, and quizzes you there
  • Gives hints, not the solution
  • Approves only when the sub-student convinces it
